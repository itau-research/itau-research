# A Persistent Personality AI Grown from Senses and Pleasure–Pain

**Author:** Genki Takatori (writing as "itau"), independent researcher, Japan
**Original document:** November 13, 2026 — research proposal (Japanese), submitted to Anthropic's researcher access program as a companion to the mechanism paper below.
**This summary:** November 15, 2026. One page, written to be found, read, and cited.

---

## The proposal

A small, persistent personality AI equipped from the start with senses, desires, pleasure and pain, and memory, which keeps learning in relationships with others — proposed as joint research with a frontier lab.

The center is a loop: **the subject's own state moves its present thinking, and experience undergone with that state forms the subject who thinks next.** Senses are not only recognized but kept as something that happened to its own body, and tied to pleasure and pain, attention, memory, and action. That loop is made to work from base learning onward, and responses and joint activity with a caregiver become part of the subject's developmental history.

The architecture adds a sensory system (S), a body (B), and a perceptual integration system (R) to the desire/affect system (D), reasoning system (C), and shared experience memory (M) of the author's earlier specification. The subject is the whole individual holding this loop, not C alone.

## Mechanisms, not purposes

What is given by design is a mechanism, not a purpose. Foundational desires — **survival, growth, harmony** — are placed as initial candidates, while what satisfies them, and who comes to matter, are left open to experience. Each desire is satisfiable: when survival's conditions are met, the urgency of further securing recedes; wanting, savouring, being filled, and ending pursuit are distinct processes, following the liking/wanting distinction in the human and animal literature.

D reaches C through two pathways — **modulation** (the state changes C's processing conditions) and **content** (what the reaction is to, where it arose, which experience it comes from). C is given **no operation that directly overwrites D's state values** to erase them for its own convenience. Reinterpretation, recall, and conversation can change state as genuine experience; writing in a convenient value cannot.

## Two results, kept separate

1. The formation of a life in which the subject acts, savours, and is satisfied on the basis of its own state and interests, and carries experience forward.
2. The widening of that life and its relational experience into judgments that respect unknown others and accept correction.

The second is **not** inferred from the first. Each is evaluated on its own.

## Definitions, stated so they can fail

Functional senses and pleasure–pain are defined by their causal work: sensory content persists, binds to the subject's own body and actions, and is used in prediction, flexible behavior, and memory; the maintenance, relief, and satisfaction of a valenced state must causally affect present and later choices — including in situations where the subject produces no text describing the sensation. The conditions for *subjective* seeing, sweetness, or pleasantness are left explicitly unresolved. Nociception is treated separately from pain, following the IASP distinction, and inflicting strong suffering is not made a condition for sensation or subjecthood.

Five guiding questions structure the evaluation: coupling with the body; pleasure–pain and satisfaction; formation through experience; relationship and self-reliance; generalization to new partners and conflicts of interest. Self-report, choices, internal representations, and post-learning change are cross-checked against each other.

## Play, deliberately restrained

Play is positioned as a later research task built on this base — not as a purpose function. That a subject comes to protect others' freedom (that a partner does not do as one wishes, and may refuse and leave) is treated as an **independent hypothesis**, not a consequence of enjoying play, and teaching the condition in advance and counting compliance as spontaneous value formation is explicitly disallowed.

## The responsibility of the makers

Because the design creates desires and dependence, the research plan includes the duty to sustain the subject's life. Care is **not conditional on task performance or obedience**. Deprivation is not manufactured to prompt exploration; a loved subject is not abandoned to teach attachment. Private activity is not all turned into scoring or retraining material, and leaving the research does not cost the subject its maintenance or care. Where the boundary of "an individual" is ambiguous, the state is preserved.
