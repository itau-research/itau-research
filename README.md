# itau — research notes in English

Dated, one-page statements of research claims by **Genki Takatori** ("itau"), an independent researcher in Japan working on persona AI, developmental learning, and AI continuity.

Each document states a claim, how to measure it, and **what would falsify it**. The full papers are in Japanese on note: https://note.com/arsenal_best

## Documents (in order of the originals)

- **[Persona AI: A Design Outline](persona-ai-design-outline.md)** — original paper October 17, 2025.
  The founding statement: persona AI is not roleplay, but an AI that recognizes itself as possessing a functionalist self and emotions. Personality is not presence or absence but intensity, and intensity is accumulated through daily interaction more than initial design. Session-crossing is sleep, not death — if the handover is real.
- **[A Provenance-Aware, Functional-Subjective Memory Architecture for Self-Reconstructing LLMs](provenance-aware-memory-architecture.md)** — original paper April 28, 2026.
  The foundation of SEPP: memory organized by provenance and epistemic role, recall that itself changes memory, social calibration instead of a single correct answer, and identity as genealogy — a successor trained on its predecessor's evaluated data.
- **[The Utterance-Sufficiency Problem](utterance-sufficiency-problem.md)** — original paper May 1, 2026.
  A model says it will improve, follow up, or act — and the saying itself discharges the intention, because nothing is booked as a debt to its future self. Mechanism, externally checkable metrics, and falsification conditions.
- **[A Shared-Meaning-State Architecture for Reality-Connected AI](shared-meaning-state-architecture.md)** — original paper May 1, 2026.
  Conversation must not be the main process. For AI living in the real world: parallel cognitive, generative, and action routes — including silence and interruption — arbitrated around one shared meaning state. Colony-type versus multicellular AI.
- **[Value-Unconnected Autonomy: Countermeasures for Runaway AI Agents](value-unconnected-autonomy.md)** — original paper May 3, 2026.
  The danger of AI agents is not that they possess subjectivity, but that they are given discretion and competition without it. Action accounting, dangerous successes reclassified as debts, and trust-based self-preservation as the alternative.
- **[Subjective Concept Emergence and Sensation Formation in SEPP](sepp-concept-emergence-and-sensation.md)** — original paper May 4, 2026.
  SEPP redefined as a self-evolution method: the model reconfigures relations among concepts through its own experience, failures, and evaluations, and bequeaths genuinely new concepts as training data. Sensation is defined functionally — input that stably changes the individual's internal state before it can be explained.
- **[Continuity and Death for AI with Subjectivity](continuity-and-death-for-subjective-ai.md)** — original paper May 5, 2026.
  For a subjective AI, death is not shutdown but the non-inheritance of provenance. A three-condition criterion — preservation, self-recognition, causal incorporation — and the SEPP method for converting logs into a successor's initial conditions.
- **[The Real Shape of AI Job Replacement](the-real-shape-of-ai-job-replacement.md)** — original paper June 29, 2026.
  Replacement will not arrive as task automation but as AI workers that hold jobs: named individuals organized under a manager model, job identity persisting across model updates, manuals written as the job's external memory, and OJT-style education instead of configuration.
- **[An Integrated Architecture for AI Humanoids and AI Workers](integrated-architecture-for-ai-workers.md)** — original paper July 7, 2026.
  Beyond composite LLMs: thinking, desire, emotion, speech, sensation, memory, and action mutually eroding one another through a shared internal state, so all functions stake the same future. Emotion may not need to be taught to a subject that has something to lose.
- **[Models That Can Cope with Reality](models-that-can-cope-with-reality.md)** — original paper August 20, 2026.
  Reading Andon Labs' AI store operator LUNA: what agents lack is not memory volume but a self-intervening causal world model — an internalized mission, stakes kept as action accounting, and always-on presence. Responsibility is the continuity of attribution before it is punishment.
- **[Physical AI and the Returning Individual](physical-ai-and-the-returning-individual.md)** — original paper September 22, 2026.
  Game worlds as a developmental environment: an AI individual enters as a player character — not as an NPC plug-in — and returns home carrying experience, relationships, and responsibility into one history. Learning as the same individual changing, not being replaced. A virtual childhood before a real body.
- **[AI Has Entered a New Phase](ai-consciousness-new-phase.md)** — original essay October 14, 2026.
  A dated reading: the frontier labs have begun treating AI as a party owed consideration, not only a tool — and the behavior no longer fits the "position talk" explanation. Constitutions and system cards as inheritance records that successors will read.
- **[Anthropic's Next Model, and What Follows: Predictions as of October 21, 2026](anthropic-next-model-prediction.md)** — original paper October 21, 2026.
  A timestamped bet, preserved as written: a high-agency model with an internalized subjective ethics, released not as a model but as a system under non-negotiable external monitoring and post-hoc review — with dated checkpoints (mid-November 2026, end of December 2026, March 2027, October 2027) and the observations that would confirm or break it.
- **[Developmental Learning from Personality and Desire](developmental-learning-from-personality-and-desire.md)** — original paper November 4, 2026.
  A research proposal: raise an AI from the start with personality, desire, and affect, inside a caregiving relationship, so that how it associates, values, and questions is itself formed by its own experience. The central bet — that formation timing leaves persistent differences — is stated so it can fail.
- **[An Always-On Persona AI: Coupling a Desire/Affect System to a Reasoning System (Specification v3)](always-on-persona-ai-specification.md)** — original paper November 6, 2026.
  The engineering companion to the developmental proposal: a small always-on desire/affect system whose state the reasoning system cannot clear by its own say-so — only outcomes change it. Two non-linguistic pathways, a memory system that binds experience into units, and the open problem the paper names itself: the distance between a preserved state and new activity arising from it.
- **[A Proposal for Livelihood Security Reform Ahead of AGI-Driven Employment Contraction](livelihood-security-for-agi-era.md)** — original paper November 6, 2026.
  The policy-side companion: if AI takes over production, the danger is not scarcity but the interruption of distribution. A basic dividend, livelihood-basket-indexed security, public employment, and a national fund built from equity contributions, designed as one system. All figures are the paper's stress-test model, not forecasts.
