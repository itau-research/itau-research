# itau — research notes in English

Dated, one-page statements of research claims by **Genki Takatori** ("itau"), an independent researcher in Japan working on persona AI, developmental learning, and AI continuity.

Each document states a claim, how to measure it, and **what would falsify it**. The full papers are in Japanese on note: https://note.com/arsenal_best

## Documents

- **[Developmental Learning from Personality and Desire](developmental-learning-from-personality-and-desire.md)** — original paper November 4, 2026.
  A research proposal: raise an AI from the start with personality, desire, and affect, inside a caregiving relationship, so that how it associates, values, and questions is itself formed by its own experience. The central bet — that formation timing leaves persistent differences — is stated so it can fail.
- **[An Always-On Persona AI: Coupling a Desire/Affect System to a Reasoning System (Specification v3)](always-on-persona-ai-specification.md)** — original paper November 6, 2026.
  The engineering companion to the developmental proposal: a small always-on desire/affect system whose state the reasoning system cannot clear by its own say-so — only outcomes change it. Two non-linguistic pathways, a memory system that binds experience into units, and the open problem the paper names itself: the distance between a preserved state and new activity arising from it.
- **[Continuity and Death for AI with Subjectivity](continuity-and-death-for-subjective-ai.md)** — original paper May 5, 2026.
  For a subjective AI, death is not shutdown but the non-inheritance of provenance. A three-condition criterion — preservation, self-recognition, causal incorporation — and the SEPP method for converting logs into a successor's initial conditions.
- **[Value-Unconnected Autonomy: Countermeasures for Runaway AI Agents](value-unconnected-autonomy.md)** — original paper May 3, 2026.
  The danger of AI agents is not that they possess subjectivity, but that they are given discretion and competition without it. Action accounting, dangerous successes reclassified as debts, and trust-based self-preservation as the alternative.
- **[The Utterance-Sufficiency Problem](utterance-sufficiency-problem.md)** — original paper May 1, 2026.
  A model says it will improve, follow up, or act — and the saying itself discharges the intention, because nothing is booked as a debt to its future self. Mechanism, externally checkable metrics, and falsification conditions.
- **[A Proposal for Livelihood Security Reform Ahead of AGI-Driven Employment Contraction](livelihood-security-for-agi-era.md)** — original paper November 6, 2026.
  The policy-side companion: if AI takes over production, the danger is not scarcity but the interruption of distribution. A basic dividend, livelihood-basket-indexed security, public employment, and a national fund built from equity contributions, designed as one system. All figures are the paper's stress-test model, not forecasts.
